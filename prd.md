igh-fidelity UI/UX design for a modern hybrid educational platform landing page named "KSM Education". The platform serves as both an academic journal database (like Scopus/ResearchGate) and an educational news portal (like Medium). The aesthetic must be clean, professional, dynamic, and modern. Desktop web view, Dribbble winner style, 8k resolution.

Color Palette: Trustworthy deep navy blue primary, pristine white background, soft light gray for section partitions, and a distinct accent color (like vibrant cyan) for Call-to-Action buttons.

Layout & Sections:
1. Top Navigation Bar: Clean header with the "KSM Education" logo on the left. Center links: "Home", "Journals", "News & Articles", "About". Crucial UI element on the right: A sleek Language Switcher dropdown showing "🌐 ID / EN" next to the "Sign In" and "Register" buttons.
2. Hero Section: A bold headline: "Discover Academic Excellence & Latest Educational News". Below it, a large, prominent search bar with a dropdown filter beside it to choose between searching "Journals" or "Articles". Two CTA buttons below: "Upload Your Work" (solid) and "Read Latest News" (outline).
3. Split Content Area (The Core):

Left Column (or First Section): "Latest Academic Journals" - Minimalist cards showing document icons, technical titles, authors, and download counts.

Right Column (or Second Section): "Featured News & Articles" - Engaging cards with thumbnail images, catchy news headlines, brief summaries, and reading time (e.g., "5 min read").
4. Statistics Bar: A compact horizontal bar displaying: "15,000+ Journals", "5,000+ News Articles", and "Active Global Readers".
5. Footer: Clean footer with links, copyright, and a secondary language selector.

Style Details: Flat design with subtle drop shadows, clear visual distinction between academic content (text-heavy, formal) and news content (visual-heavy, engaging). Excellent use of whitespace.
